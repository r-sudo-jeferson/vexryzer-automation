## Founder decision

The Founder explicitly approved the Agent-Led Accounting Seller design and instructed Engineering to execute it.

Engineering is authorized to implement, inside the already-authorized `VXA-S002@1.0.0`, an accounting-firm-specialized Seller with maximum practical improvisational freedom and strong quantitative persuasion, bounded by deterministic truth/safety/execution controls.

## Authorized behavioral change

The Seller is no longer to be implemented as a next-question-centric flow, rigid solution classifier, fixed scene sequence, scripted objection tree or deterministic funnel.

The Seller may choose and combine questions, insights, challenges, verified calculations, comparisons, visual changes, artifacts, capability combinations, objection responses and Workshop demonstrations according to the strongest truthful next move.

The experience must react to accepted strategy, not merely display a transcript beside a static graph.

## Quantitative mandate

Engineering must make quantitative persuasion a first-class capability. Where evidence permits, the Seller should translate accounting-office friction into concrete time, volume, capacity, recurring burden, closing pressure, rework or other economically meaningful quantities.

Authoritative arithmetic remains deterministic and provenance-bound. The authorization does not permit invented ROI, savings, cost, payback, percentages, feasibility, tax exposure or urgency.

## Vertical and capability mandate

The primary vertical is accounting firms. The experience may freely combine operational/digital artifacts, imports/transforms, presentations, BI/decision intelligence, training/enablement, automation/integrations, internal tools, AI/agentic systems and process/data improvements.

These capabilities must not become a mandatory single-category sales classifier.
