> This amendment is Founder-review material. It does not change the active Product Contract until explicitly authorized.

## Amendment intent

Expand Vexryzer Automation from an automation-only intake framing into an adaptive solution-commerce experience without weakening the existing truthfulness, security, pricing-authority, persistence or non-degradation rules.

The product remains a professional commercial intake and discovery experience. It does not become a free implementation engine, generic chatbot, unrestricted code generator, marketplace or autonomous production deployment system.

## Amended North Star

Transform an operational pain, repetitive process, decision bottleneck or capability gap into a qualified solution opportunity in the fewest responsible interactions while making the visitor visibly experience that their operation is being understood, structured and improved in front of them.

## Primary outcome

Primary outcome becomes `Qualified Solution Opportunity Submission Rate`.

A qualified solution opportunity contains enough verified or explicitly uncertain information to route the request responsibly into one or more solution classes without forcing a traditional long form or pretending technical feasibility is known.

## Supported solution classes

ASK AI may identify, compare and qualify the following classes:

- workflow automation and integrations;
- micro-SaaS and productized internal tools;
- BI, dashboards, reporting and decision intelligence;
- AI-assisted and agentic operational systems;
- data/process improvements that are prerequisites to automation;
- training, enablement and operational education;
- hybrid solutions combining the above;
- `no_software_fit` when software is not currently the responsible recommendation.

A solution class is a hypothesis until validated. Attractive framing never upgrades a hypothesis into a guarantee.

## ASK AI Experience Truth

ASK AI is the central intelligence and interaction nucleus of the product, not a floating assistant placed beside an otherwise independent page.

The surrounding UI, Infinite Canvas, camera, process topology, uncertainty, opportunity framing and prototype surfaces react to validated understanding produced during the conversation.

ASK AI defaults to one principal question at a time. It may use chips, visual choices, node selection, micro-inputs or comparisons when they reduce cognitive load. It must not require diagramming knowledge.

## Infinite Canvas Product Truth

The Infinite Canvas is the visible proof-of-understanding substrate. It may progressively represent:

- actors and systems;
- sources, handoffs, transformations and outputs;
- repetition, friction, uncertainty and dependencies;
- multiple processes and shared systems;
- competing or complementary solution directions;
- before/after concepts;
- prototype demonstrations;
- confidence and provenance;
- the next commercial/discovery decision.

The Canvas must increase clarity, not create a diagramming task. Core progress remains possible without manual pan, zoom or spatial expertise.

## Adaptive UI authority

The model may propose a typed visual scene. It may not author or execute arbitrary DOM, CSS, JavaScript, JSX or unbounded component imports in the customer runtime.

Application code owns:

- scene-schema validation;
- component allowlisting;
- design tokens;
- layout constraints;
- camera resolution;
- accessibility behavior;
- content bounds;
- motion policy;
- executable-code boundaries.

Unknown visual component kinds fail closed.

## Prototype doctrine

A prototype exists to demonstrate understanding and improve commercial clarity. It is not proof that the final solution has been implemented or deployed.

Every prototype shown to a visitor must be visibly and semantically distinguishable from production functionality when confusion is reasonably possible.

A live development agent may create prototype artifacts only behind an isolated capability boundary. Generated executable code must never run directly in the same-origin customer application merely because a model produced it.

## Persuasion doctrine

Persuasion is earned through clarity, demonstrated understanding, credible synthesis, useful comparison and appropriate demonstration.

ASK AI may:

- expose hidden repetition or coordination cost using user-provided facts;
- explain why a solution class may fit;
- compare solution directions;
- show a bounded concept or prototype;
- highlight uncertainty honestly;
- ask for a next commercial action.

ASK AI must not use:

- fake scarcity;
- false urgency;
- fabricated ROI;
- fake testimonials or logos;
- invented compatibility;
- unapproved discounts;
- unsupported feasibility claims;
- manipulative fear, shame or pressure;
- a fabricated progress animation pretending work occurred.

## Commercial boundary

ASK AI should be useful enough to establish competence without delivering the paid implementation itself as free pre-sales consulting.

It may provide conceptual framing, process insight, high-level architecture and a controlled demonstration. It must not produce a deployable customer solution, production credentials, unrestricted source package or complete implementation recipe when that would substitute for the paid engagement.

## Training and enablement

Training is a first-class solution class when the evidence supports it, not a forced cross-sell.

ASK AI may recommend training alone or as part of a hybrid solution when the dominant constraint is process adoption, operational skill, data practice or safe use of automation/AI rather than missing software.

## No-fit behavior

The product must be capable of concluding that automation or software is not currently the responsible next step. `no_software_fit` is a valid high-integrity outcome and must not be suppressed for conversion optics.

## Minimum sufficient brief evolution

The common minimum brief remains intentionally small:

- pain/process or capability gap;
- desired outcome;
- recurrence/frequency when relevant;
- people/roles affected when material;
- systems/data involved when material;
- known constraints or examples when material;
- uncertainty that blocks responsible qualification;
- contact information only at the appropriate later commercial step.

Each solution class may require additional class-specific facts, but ASK AI must not collect them before they become decision-relevant.

## Pricing boundary

S002 does not move pricing authority into the LLM.

The existing recurring-automation pricing formula remains authoritative only where the active pricing contract explicitly applies it. It must not be silently reused for micro-SaaS, BI, training, agentic systems or other classes whose economics differ.

Any broader pricing model requires a later explicit Product Contract decision and deterministic server-side implementation.

## State and AI authority

Existing AI authority restrictions remain in force and are strengthened:

- AI proposes language, structured facts, solution hypotheses, scene intents and tool requests;
- deterministic application code validates and applies material state;
- AI cannot seal requests, confirm uploads, grant discounts, set authoritative price, elevate its own tool permissions or mark technical feasibility confirmed without evidence;
- an independent Critic may block or request revision of material AI proposals, but the Critic also cannot override deterministic authority.

## Failure truth

No external model, Workshop or prototype service may become a single point of failure for the commercial discovery path.

If AI or Workshop capability is slow or unavailable, the experience preserves confirmed understanding and continues through a deterministic guided fallback or approved generative-UI path.

No indefinite spinner. No fake progress percentage.

## Privacy and provider state

Normalized application state remains the product source of truth. Provider-side conversation persistence, if used, is an implementation aid and not authoritative state.

Provider-side storage must be explicitly evaluated against privacy requirements; the default planning posture is to avoid unnecessary provider persistence and avoid sending data that the current task does not require.

## Non-degradation

This amendment does not weaken existing rules for accessibility, mobile usability, performance, security, deterministic pricing, durable submission semantics, upload truth, failure recovery, evidence handling or user control of the Canvas.

## Adoption gate

This amendment becomes active only through explicit Founder authorization tied to a concrete Slice contract and GAUNTLET. Until then, the active automation-focused Product Contract remains the repository contract.
